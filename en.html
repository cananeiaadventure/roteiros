A full day with Patrick

On board with the chef

A day on the boat with Patrick, captain and chef: from the fish caught in the Lagamar to sushi and sashimi, made on board or on one of the islands. Forget classes between four walls: here you learn by doing.

  1. Morning on boardA boat trip around the area. If you like, dive with Patrick to catch the fish for dinner; if you'd rather, fish with a rod.
  2. IkejimeThe Japanese technique for killing fish: how it's done, what it's for and why it makes a difference at the table.
  3. Tasting on boardSeafood from whatever we manage to catch and whatever nature offers that day.
  4. BreakTime to rest and recharge.
  5. Prep on board or on the islandHandling the fish for dry-aging, sushi and sashimi, on the boat or on one of the islands. And to finish, one more tasting.

Certificate included.

About us

Your hosts in the Lagamar

Giu and Patrick grew up in Cananéia, each in their own way. Patrick knows the Lagamar from the boat, from diving and from surfing: the tides, the estuary, fishing and fish, from the sea to the plate. Giu knows it through her eye: the best scenes and moments, the curious facts and stories of the town, and the trails from her years as an environmental monitor. On the trips, Giu is your host: she shares the stories she has heard since she was little and guides the eye of anyone who wants to take photos and really notice the place.

Our trips grow out of what we already do every day, and each outing is adjusted to the tide, the wind, you and your group.

Easy booking

How booking works

  1. Ask for a quoteBuild your day with the quiz, or pick a trip and send it to us on WhatsApp.
  2. We talk it throughWe agree on the date, route and price based on the tide, the weather and your group.
  3. Booking confirmedWith a 50% deposit by Pix or Wise, the date is yours. The boat is booked for your group.

If you need to cancel or the weather doesn't cooperate

  • Up to 7 days before: we refund the full deposit.
  • From 7 days to 48 hours before: the deposit becomes credit to rebook in the same season.
  • Less than 48 hours before, or no-show: the deposit is not refunded, because the boat was already booked for your group.
  • Bad weather or rough seas: if we cancel for your safety, you choose: rebook at no cost or get your deposit back.

Before you come

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

In high season, yes. The boat is booked ahead of time, so the sooner you message us, the easier it is to find the right date and tide.

How do I confirm my booking?

Once we agree on the date, route and price on WhatsApp, your booking is confirmed with a 50% deposit by Pix or Wise. The cancellation rules are under How booking works.

Does the trip depend on the tide and weather?

It does. We choose the departure time and the stops based on the day's tide and wind, and agree on everything with you beforehand.

What if it rains?

We keep an eye on the forecast together with you. If the weather or the sea won't let us go out safely, you choose: rebook at no cost or get your deposit back.

Can I bring children?

Children are welcome. Tell us their ages on WhatsApp and we'll suggest the right trip.

Are there life jackets?

Yes, there are life jackets for everyone on board.

Where do we meet?

At Cananéia's Municipal Pier. We set the time according to the tide.

Is there food on the trip?

You can add the bento on board, made by Patrick, as an extra. Some trips can also stop for lunch at local restaurants. We'll sort it all out with you on WhatsApp.

Do you take photos and videos of the trip?

We do, as a separate service. Giu captures your day in photos and videos. Ask when you book and we'll send you the price.

What should I bring?

Sunscreen, insect repellent, a cap, swimwear, water and a change of clothes. For trails, shoes that can get wet.
